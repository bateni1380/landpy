🚀 Meet Landpy—my open-source Python package for building image-based augmented reality effects.

Give a face a new look. Add a transparent sticker. Make an overlay follow a tilted surface. Turn landmarks into masks and bring your ideas into the image. 🎨✨

One of my favorite ideas inside it: retrace()—edit a crop, then bring the effect back to its original place. ⏪

📓 Want to see it in action? Eight step-by-step notebooks take you from your first edit to a complete face effect, with visual examples you can run in Colab.

This project sat unfinished for years. AI helped me finally finish it. Thank you, ChatGPT, for helping turn an old idea into something people can install, explore, and build with. 🤖❤️
